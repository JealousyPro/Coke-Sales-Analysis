# Coke-Sales-Analysis
📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Coca-Cola sales data using Microsoft Excel, focusing on sales performance, product trends, regional distribution, and profitability. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data cleaning, calculated fields, and pivot table analysis.

The project demonstrates practical skills in:

✅ Data Cleaning
✅ Excel Formulas
✅ Pivot Tables
✅ Sales Analysis
✅ Business Intelligence Reporting
✅ Business Insights Generation

🎯 Project Objectives

🔹 Analyze monthly sales trends throughout the year.

🔹 Compare performance across different beverage brands.

🔹 Evaluate regional sales distribution.

🔹 Measure profitability and operating margins.

🔹 Generate actionable business recommendations.

📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains sales records from various Coca-Cola retailers across multiple regions.

📋 Dataset Fields

🔸 Retailer
🔸 Retailer ID
🔸 Invoice Date
🔸 Region
🔸 State
🔸 City
🔸 Beverage Brand
🔸 Price per Unit
🔸 Units Sold
🔸 Total Sales
🔸 Operating Profit
🔸 Operating Margin
🔸 Month

🧮 Calculated Columns
📅 Month Extraction
=MONTH(Invoice Date)
💰 Total Sales
=Price per Unit * Units Sold
📈 Operating Profit
=Total Sales * Operating Margin

📊 Intermediate Level Analysis
🎯 Goal

Compare sales performance across beverage brands.

📌 Pivot Table Setup

🔹 Rows → Month

🔹 Columns → Beverage Brand

🔹 Values → Sum of Total Sales

🥤 Brands Analyzed

🟥 Coca-Cola

🟦 Dasani Water

🟨 Diet Coke

🟧 Fanta

🟩 Powerade

🟪 Sprite

📈 Key Findings

🟢 Coca-Cola consistently generated the highest revenue.

🟢 Dasani Water showed strong summer demand.

🟢 Powerade sales increased during warmer months.

🟢 Fanta contributed lower revenue compared to other brands.

🟢 Most brands experienced sales spikes between June and August.

💡 Business Insight

📍 Seasonal promotions can be customized by product category.

📍 Inventory planning should align with seasonal demand patterns.

📍 High-performing products should receive greater marketing focus.

📈 Key Findings

💡 Business Insight

📍 Regional marketing campaigns can improve lower-performing regions.

📍 Demand forecasting can be optimized using seasonal trends.
